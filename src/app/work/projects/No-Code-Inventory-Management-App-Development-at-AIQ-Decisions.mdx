## Overview

During my internship at AIQ Decisions, I led the design and implementation of a no-code inventory management application aimed at providing real-time stock level tracking and automating essential inventory processes. Leveraging AppSheet's no-code platform, I built a user-friendly app that streamlined purchase orders, supplier notifications, and order management, significantly enhancing operational efficiency.

The app seamlessly integrated with external data sources such as Google Sheets and BigQuery for real-time reporting and analytics, providing valuable insights and facilitating data-driven decision-making.

## Key Features

- **Intuitive App Interface**: Crafted a clean, accessible UI to allow users across different roles and devices to monitor and manage inventory levels effortlessly.
- **Process Automation**: Automated workflows for purchase orders, supplier notifications, and order tracking to reduce manual effort and minimize errors.
- **Data Sync & Integration**: Utilized AppSheet’s powerful data connectors to sync inventory data in real time with Google Sheets and BigQuery, enabling up-to-date analytics and reporting.
- **Cross-Device Compatibility**: Customized UI/UX for seamless experience across desktop and mobile devices, ensuring accessibility and usability in various environments.
- **Rigorous Testing & Debugging**: Conducted thorough testing cycles to validate business logic, scalability, and prediction accuracy, ensuring the app met the operational needs.
- **Cost Efficiency**: Delivered a solution that cut costs by minimizing dependency on expensive third-party inventory management platforms like Zoho Inventory and NetSuite.

## Technologies Used

- **AppSheet**: No-code platform for app building, data connectors, and workflow automation.
- **Google Sheets & BigQuery**: Backend data storage and real-time analytics integration.
- **Custom UI/UX Design**: Tailored within AppSheet for enhanced usability.

## Challenges and Learnings

One of the key challenges was designing workflows that accurately reflected complex inventory processes without custom coding. Balancing automation with flexibility required careful configuration of AppSheet's rules and data sync mechanisms.

Ensuring real-time data accuracy and consistency across multiple platforms (Google Sheets, BigQuery, and the app itself) demanded extensive testing and fine-tuning. Additionally, creating a UI that was both functional and user-friendly across devices pushed me to deeply understand AppSheet’s customization capabilities.

## Outcome

The no-code inventory app significantly improved stock management efficiency at AIQ Decisions, streamlining key operational processes and enhancing real-time visibility. By reducing reliance on costly third-party tools, the solution provided substantial cost savings while maintaining scalability and accuracy.

This project sharpened my skills in rapid prototyping, no-code tool utilization, and cross-platform data integration, demonstrating how no-code solutions can deliver high-impact business value quickly and cost-effectively.
