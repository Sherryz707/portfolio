## Overview

During my internship at AIQ Decisions, I spearheaded the development of a no-code inventory management application designed to deliver real-time stock tracking and automate critical inventory workflows. Utilizing AppSheet’s no-code platform, I crafted a highly intuitive app that streamlined key processes such as purchase order generation, supplier communication, and order status management—resulting in significantly improved operational efficiency and accuracy.

The solution seamlessly integrated with external data ecosystems including Google Sheets and BigQuery, providing powerful real-time analytics and enabling stakeholders to make timely, data-driven decisions with confidence.

## Key Features

- **User-Centric Interface**: Developed a clean, role-based UI optimized for both desktop and mobile, ensuring accessibility and ease of use for diverse teams.
- **Workflow Automation**: Automated routine inventory tasks including purchase order creation, supplier notifications, and stock level alerts, drastically reducing manual effort and errors.
- **Robust Data Integration**: Leveraged AppSheet’s connectors to maintain synchronized, real-time data flows between the app, Google Sheets, and BigQuery for comprehensive reporting and monitoring.
- **Cross-Platform Usability**: Ensured seamless user experience across multiple devices and operating environments to support operational agility.
- **Quality Assurance**: Conducted iterative testing and debugging to validate business rules, ensure data consistency, and guarantee app scalability under real-world conditions.
- **Cost-Effective Solution**: Delivered a powerful alternative to costly third-party inventory systems, cutting operational expenses while maintaining enterprise-grade functionality.

## Technologies Used

- **AppSheet**: No-code platform enabling rapid app development and workflow automation.
- **Google Sheets & BigQuery**: Backend data storage and real-time analytics for informed decision-making.
- **Custom UI/UX**: Tailored within AppSheet to balance functionality with user-friendliness.

## Challenges and Learnings

Translating complex inventory workflows into no-code logic required deep understanding of AppSheet’s automation rules and data synchronization capabilities. Achieving real-time accuracy across distributed data sources involved rigorous testing and meticulous troubleshooting.

Designing an interface that was both comprehensive and intuitive across device types pushed me to explore and maximize AppSheet’s customization options. This project strengthened my ability to deliver scalable business solutions rapidly using no-code tools while maintaining high standards of data integrity and user experience.

## Outcome

The deployed no-code inventory management app dramatically improved stock visibility and process automation at AIQ Decisions. It reduced manual workload, minimized errors, and empowered teams with actionable real-time insights—all while eliminating the need for expensive legacy inventory software.

This project showcased the transformative potential of no-code platforms in accelerating digital transformation and operational efficiency, especially for businesses prioritizing speed, flexibility, and cost-effectiveness.
